Your blood pressure, part of your fitness routine.

A strong heart powers an active life, and your blood pressure is one of its clearest signs. This app makes it effortless to log your numbers, watch your trends, and see how your workouts, rest, and habits move them — in seconds a day, free, with no account.

🏃 Built for an active lifestyle
Blood pressure is a core wellness metric, right alongside your steps, workouts, and sleep. Log it in seconds and keep it part of the routine that keeps you moving.

✏️ Log your readings for free, in seconds
Fast, tap-friendly sliders let you set systolic, diastolic, and pulse in moments. Completely free, works offline, and needs no account — start tracking the very first time you open the app.

📷 Or scan your monitor with the camera (Premium)
Don't want to type at all? Photograph your home monitor and the numbers are read for you automatically. Camera scanning is an optional Premium feature for the easiest possible entry.

📈 Watch your trends
Interactive charts reveal how your numbers move over days, weeks, and months. Smart "Avg" smoothing combines back-to-back readings into a single, honest data point — so your trend line reflects reality.

🏷️ See what moves your numbers
Tag any reading with context — exercise, caffeine, a salty meal, poor sleep, stress — and discover how your activity and lifestyle shape your blood pressure over time.

❤️ Connects with Health Connect
Sync with Android Health Connect to keep your blood pressure right alongside your activity, workouts, and other fitness data — on your terms, with your permission.

📄 Keep a clean PDF record
Export a clear, professional PDF of your history — charts and readings included — to keep for yourself or share with whomever you choose.

⏰ Build a consistent habit
Optional gentle reminders help you log at the same time each morning and evening, so your trends stay complete.

🔒 Private by design
Your data stays on your device. No account to create, no cloud sign-up, nothing sold or shared. Your numbers are your business — and only yours.

Features at a glance
• Free, fast manual logging with sliders — no account needed
