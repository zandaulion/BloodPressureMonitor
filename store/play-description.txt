Take control of your blood pressure — in seconds a day.

Blood pressure matters, but logging it shouldn't be a chore. Add a reading in seconds — free, with no account — then watch your trends take shape and share a clean report with your doctor in one tap. No spreadsheets, no hassle.

✏️ Log your readings for free, in seconds
Entering a reading is effortless: fast, tap-friendly sliders let you set systolic, diastolic, and pulse in moments. It's completely free, works offline, and needs no account — start tracking the very first time you open the app.

📷 Or scan your monitor with the camera (Premium)
Don't want to type at all? Photograph your blood pressure monitor and the numbers are read for you automatically. Camera scanning is an optional Premium feature for those who want the easiest possible entry.

📈 See what your numbers mean
Every reading is sorted into its blood pressure category, so you instantly know where you stand. Interactive charts reveal your trends over days, weeks, or months. Smart "Avg" smoothing combines back-to-back measurements into a single, honest data point.

📄 Doctor-ready PDF reports
Export a clear, professional report of your history — charts and readings included — and share it with your doctor by email, message, or print. Perfect for check-ups and consultations.

🔒 Private by design
Your health data stays on your device. No account to create, no cloud sign-up required, nothing sold or shared. Your blood pressure is your business — and only yours.

❤️ Connects with Health Connect
Sync your readings with Android Health Connect to keep your blood pressure alongside the rest of your health data — on your terms, with your permission.

⏰ Never forget a reading
Optional gentle reminders help you measure at the same time each morning and evening, so your trends stay accurate and complete.
