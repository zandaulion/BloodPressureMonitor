BP Digitizer is a private, local-first log for your blood pressure readings. Point the camera at your digital monitor and the numbers are read on your phone, or enter them by hand.

<b>Works offline, no account</b>
The app has no Internet permission. There is no account, subscription, cloud service or model download. Android's own backup to Google Drive and its phone-to-phone transfer are turned off for this app, so your readings and profile stay only on this phone. To keep a copy or move to a new phone, export a JSON backup.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>On-device camera scanning: the bundled Hearth models read SYS, DIA and pulse from an upright digital monitor with three rows. You always review the values before saving.</li>
<li>Manual entry with sliders, starting from your last reading</li>
<li>Trend chart with 7-day, 30-day, 90-day and all-time ranges, pinch-to-zoom, and a rotatable 3D scatter of systolic, diastolic and pulse</li>
<li>Landscape view with the trend chart and scatter side by side</li>
<li>Context tags such as caffeine, exercise, a salty meal or medication</li>
<li>Two daily reminders with snooze</li>
<li>Export as JSON backup, CSV or a multi-page PDF report; import Android and BP Digitizer web app backups</li>
<li>Optional health profile used to show AHA-based reference categories</li>
<li>12 languages: English, Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Romanian, Spanish and Ukrainian</li>
</ul>

<b>Health Connect (optional)</b>
You can copy your readings to Health Connect from the dashboard. This is never required. On Android 14 and later Health Connect is part of the system; on Android 13 and below it needs Google's proprietary Health Connect app.

<b>Requirements</b>
Release builds support ARM64 (arm64-v8a) phones running Android 8.0 or later.

<b>Not a medical device.</b> This app is for personal record-keeping only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your blood pressure.
